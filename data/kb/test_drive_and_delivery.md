# Test drives and delivery

## Test drives

Test drives are free and last 45 minutes. They are offered at Velora studios in Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Cologne and Frankfurt. Drivers must be at least 21 years old and bring a valid driving licence. Test drives can be booked up to 30 days in advance via the app, the website or the assistant.

## Home test drive

In Berlin and Munich, Velora can bring the car to your home for a test drive. A home test drive costs 49 €, which is refunded if you order a car within 30 days.

## Ordering and delivery

The current delivery time is 8 to 12 weeks after order. An order can be cancelled free of charge within 14 days. Cars are delivered to the nearest Velora studio or, for 390 €, to your home.
