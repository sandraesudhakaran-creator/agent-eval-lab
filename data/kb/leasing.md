# Leasing and financing

## Private leasing examples

Example offers for private customers with a 36-month term, 10,000 km per year and no down payment:

- Pulse: 399 € per month
- Arc: 529 € per month
- Ridge: 649 € per month

Each additional kilometre beyond the agreed mileage costs 0.12 €.

## Requirements

Leasing requires a residence in Germany and a positive credit check. Business leasing for companies and self-employed customers is also available on request.

## Financing

Velora offers financing with terms from 12 to 60 months. The current effective annual interest rate is 4.9 %.
