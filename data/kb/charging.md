# Charging

## Connectors and onboard charger

All Velora models use the CCS2 connector for DC fast charging and Type 2 for AC charging. Every model has an 11 kW AC onboard charger. A 22 kW onboard charger is available as an option for the Arc and the Ridge for 790 €; it is not available for the Pulse.

## Home charging

The Velora HomeCharge wallbox charges at up to 11 kW. It costs 1,190 € including standard installation. Standard installation covers up to 10 metres of cable from the fuse box.

## Public charging

With the Velora Charge card, customers pay 0.39 € per kWh at all partner fast chargers in Europe. There is no monthly fee for the first 12 months; afterwards the card costs 4.99 € per month.

## Charging tips

For daily driving, Velora recommends charging to 80 %. Charge to 100 % only before long trips. Battery preconditioning can be started from the Velora app, which shortens fast-charging times in winter.
