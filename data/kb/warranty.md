# Warranty

All Velora vehicles are covered by a vehicle warranty of 3 years or 100,000 km, whichever comes first.

## Battery warranty

The high-voltage battery is covered for 8 years or 160,000 km, whichever comes first. Velora guarantees that the battery keeps at least 70 % of its original usable capacity during this period. If capacity drops below 70 %, the battery is repaired or replaced free of charge.

## Paint and corrosion

Paint defects are covered for 3 years. Body corrosion (rust perforation) is covered for 12 years.

## Transfer to a new owner

The vehicle and battery warranties are tied to the car, not to the owner. They transfer automatically when the car is sold.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage from accidents, racing or track use, unauthorised modifications, or normal wear parts such as tyres, brake pads and wiper blades.
