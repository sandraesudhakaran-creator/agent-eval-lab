# Service and maintenance

## Service interval

A Velora needs a service every 2 years or 30,000 km, whichever comes first. Electric cars need no oil changes; the service checks brakes, coolant, cabin filter, tyres and software.

## Velora Care plans

Velora Care Basic costs 19 € per month and covers all scheduled services.

Velora Care Plus costs 39 € per month and covers all scheduled services plus wear parts: brake pads, brake discs, wiper blades and one set of tyres.

Both plans have a minimum term of 36 months.

## Roadside assistance

Roadside assistance is included free of charge for the first 3 years. With Velora Care Plus it is extended for as long as the plan runs. The hotline is available 24 hours a day in all EU countries.
