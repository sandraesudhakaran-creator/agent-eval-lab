# Software, assistance systems and app

## Over-the-air updates

Velora vehicles receive over-the-air software updates about once a month. Updates install while the car is parked and take around 20 minutes.

## Driver assistance

Velora Assist is standard on every model: adaptive cruise control, lane keeping and emergency braking. Velora Assist Pro adds automatic lane changes on motorways and costs 9.90 € per month.

## Velora app

The app offers remote climate control, charge scheduling, battery preconditioning and a digital key for up to 5 drivers. The digital key works on iOS and Android.

## Data privacy

Vehicle and app data are stored on servers in the European Union. Customers can download or delete their data in the app at any time.
